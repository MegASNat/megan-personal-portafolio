02about me

The invisible string.

For a long time I felt behind. I'd be making progress and still feel like I wasn't doing the things that actually mattered to me. But looking back, the things I chose — on instinct, sometimes just by avoiding what I didn't want — turned out to be the right ones. It's like an invisible string. You only see it looking backwards.

I used to wish I could be a machine. 100% productive, no sleep, no breaks, just working. I'm glad I didn't get that wish — it would've deleted everything I actually like about being alive. So instead I build. Usually software, sometimes hardware, mostly for people the tech world tends to skip. I take the road less traveled, with a little prudence, and I'm learning to trust that the effort adds up.

what runs through my work

  • People-centered engineeringbuild for a real person first, then for everyone
  • Accessibilityfor the people the tech world tends to skip
  • Autonomytools that help people do it themselves
